ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Wakatayi Senior Secondary School is an educational institution located in Luwero, Bamunanika, Zirobwe-Wakataayi, Uganda. The school provides secular education, Islamic theology studies, vocational training and co-curricular activities for learners. Our motto is "Education Is My Shield".

OUR PROGRAMMES

SECULAR STUDIES

Arts and Sciences at Ordinary Level and Advanced Level.

THEOLOGY STUDIES

Shuuba, Idaad, Thanawi, Islamic Da'wah and Quran memorisation for girls and boys.

VOCATIONAL STUDIES

Tailoring, ICT, farming, agriculture, welding and plumbing.

CO-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES

Sports and other activities that develop learners' talents.
